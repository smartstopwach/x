How to play

  1. 1
    Pick a level. Easy has 6 pairs, Medium 8, Hard 12.
  2. 2
    Flip two cards. If they match, they stay open. If not, they flip back.
  3. 3
    Clear the board. Fewer moves and less time means a better score.

Install it on your phone

No app store needed. Add it to your home screen in a few taps.

🍏 iPhone & iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button.
  3. Choose Add to Home Screen.

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu ⋮.
  3. Choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
